Methodology & formulas
  • Expiring Premium = Expiring ROL × Limit
  • New RA Flat Premium = New Expected Loss + (Expiring MSD × New Standard Deviation) — the expiring margin on standard deviation (MSD) is held constant and applied to this year's expected loss and standard deviation to derive a risk-adjusted flat technical price.
  • New RA Flat ROL = New RA Flat Premium / Limit
  • Rate Change = New RA Flat ROL / Expiring ROL − 1

Margin on Standard Deviation (MSD) is the risk margin per unit of loss standard deviation implied by (or agreed for) the expiring placement: (Expiring Premium − Expiring Expected Loss) / Expiring Standard Deviation. Enter it directly per layer — from the prior year's pricing file, a market benchmark, or your own calculation. Holding MSD flat year-over-year is what makes this a "risk-adjusted flat" reprice: any change in the resulting rate on line is driven purely by the change in modeled expected loss and standard deviation, not by a change in risk appetite.
